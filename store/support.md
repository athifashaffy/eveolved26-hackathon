## Common actions

- Connect data: open **Profile > Connected Devices**, review the categories,
  then tap **Connect health data**.
- Keep using Signa without health access: optional manual check-ins remain
  available, but a wearable-based score may remain unavailable.
- Prepare a clinic visit: open **Profile > Clinician wellness summary** and
  choose whether to share the generated PDF.
- Delete everything: open **Profile > Erase all data**, read the warning, and
  confirm **Erase permanently**. No account-support request is needed.
- Revoke access: use Apple Health, Health Connect, or Fitbit account settings.

Signa does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication, or emergency support.
For urgent risk, call local emergency services; 988 is available by call or
text in Canada and the United States.
