## Short description

Private, explainable wellness patterns from the health data you choose.

## Full description

Signa learns your personal wellness baseline from available HRV, sleep, steps,
and optional mood check-ins. Review a confidence-labelled daily view, actual
7/30/90-day trends, and context categories. Ask Signa provides constrained
on-phone explanations. You control if and when a wellness PDF is shared.

Signa is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, recommend
medication, monitor emergencies, or alert a clinic.

## Data safety

- Account creation: no.
- Data shared by the developer: no.
- Data collected by the developer: no health values, profile data, user
  content, identifiers, diagnostics, or location in the current release.
- Encryption: device/OS protections apply to local storage; Fitbit uses HTTPS.
- Deletion: immediate in-app **Erase all data**; uninstall also removes app data.
- Advertising and tracking: none.

## Health apps / Health Connect declaration

- Types: read Heart Rate Variability, Sleep, and Steps only.
- Purpose: personalized wellness baseline, explainable trend views, and
  user-requested reports.
- Permissions are requested only after an explanation and explicit action.
- No write permissions and no use for advertising, eligibility, credit, or
  insurance.

AI-generated content is constrained to supplied facts, filtered before display,
and bypassed for crisis, diagnosis/treatment, and medication requests. The
store candidate keeps the unverified native Gemma runtime disabled.
