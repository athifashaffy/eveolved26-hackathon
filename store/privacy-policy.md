# Signa Privacy Policy

Effective: July 13, 2026  
Responsible entity: **[LEGAL OWNER NAME REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION]**

Signa is an account-free personal wellness app for adults 18 and older. It
helps you understand how available sleep, HRV, activity, and optional mood
signals move relative to your own usual range. Signa is not a medical device,
does not diagnose or treat a condition, and does not monitor emergencies.

## Data and purposes

With explicit permission, Signa reads HRV, sleep, and steps from Apple Health,
Health Connect, or Fitbit. It stores those readings, personal baseline scores,
confidence, optional check-ins, context markers, and profile fields locally to
produce the Today and Trends views. Fitbit authorization uses Fitbit's OAuth
and API services; Apple Health and Health Connect are operating-system services.

Signa does not create a cloud account. Signa does not upload health values,
scores, profile fields, notes, questions, answers, or advertising identifiers
to a Signa server. There is no advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale of data.

## Private coach

Ask Signa receives only reviewed derived facts such as score confidence and
approved contributor explanations. It does not receive raw wearable records,
profile fields, OAuth tokens, export files, or private notes. Its conversation
is held in memory and cleared when the screen closes or the app backgrounds.
When the optional Gemma model is enabled after device verification, a static
signed model package is downloaded without health or conversation content;
inference then runs offline on the phone. Until that release gate passes, Ask
Signa uses the deterministic on-device explanation.

## Sharing and clinics

Nothing is automatically sent to a clinic. You may create a PDF, CSV, or JSON
file and choose a recipient using your phone's share sheet. The clinician PDF
excludes free-text context notes. After sharing, the chosen recipient controls
their copy.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains on the phone until you use **Profile > Erase all data** or remove
the app. Erase all data removes the profile, readings, scores, check-ins, notes,
device tokens, settings, and downloaded coach model. Revoking Apple Health,
Health Connect, or Fitbit access stops future retrieval.
