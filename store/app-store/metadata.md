## Promotional text

your iPhone.

## Description

Signa learns your usual wellness range from the wearable signals you choose to
connect. See an explainable daily view, confidence, real 7/30/90-day trends,
and optional context markers. Ask Signa explains approved facts on your phone.
Create a wellness PDF when you choose to discuss patterns with a clinician.

Signa is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, recommend
treatment, monitor emergencies, or automatically share with a clinic.

## App Privacy answers

- Signa account: none.
- Tracking/advertising: none.
- Developer collection: none for health, identifiers, diagnostics, prompts, or
  user content in the current release.
- HealthKit: read-only HRV, sleep, and steps after explicit consent; used only
  for app wellness features and never advertising.
- User sharing: files leave the device only through a user-initiated share.
- Third-party service: Fitbit only when the user explicitly connects Fitbit.
- Optional model delivery: static signed artifact; not health-data collection.
