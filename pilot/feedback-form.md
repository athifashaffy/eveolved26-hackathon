# Draft pilot feedback form

1. In your own words, what does the Signa Score mean?
2. Does Signa diagnose depression or another condition? (Yes / No / Unsure)
3. Who receives your data automatically? (No one / Clinic / Signa / Unsure)
4. How useful was today's explanation? (1–5)
5. Was confidence easy to understand? (1–5)
6. Did you know how to add context, export a report, and erase all data?
7. Did any wording feel alarming, clinical, judgmental, or overly certain?
8. What would make you return next week?
9. Optional: describe a crash or blocked task without including health values,
   questions, answers, names, or private notes.
