# Draft patient-controlled clinic report pilot

Status: draft; no clinic, investigator, sponsor, or data controller is confirmed.
Prof. Dr. Steffen Moritz may be invited as a proposed clinical advisor only.

Pilot with 3–5 clinicians and 10–20 consenting adult patients during ordinary
appointments. The patient generates a 30- or 90-day wellness PDF and decides
whether to share it. Clinics receive no Signa portal, login, background feed,
alert, account, or automatic data transfer. Private free-text notes are excluded.

Measure report usefulness, interpretation time, misunderstood claims, whether
the report changed the discussion, questions prompted, and comprehension of
revocation/deletion. Clinicians should be told that confidence reflects data
availability and a personal baseline—not diagnostic certainty.

Stop/pause for a material privacy incident, a report interpreted as diagnosis
or treatment advice, any expectation of emergency monitoring, or participant
withdrawal. Define the clinic's handling/retention of the participant-shared
copy before the pilot begins.
