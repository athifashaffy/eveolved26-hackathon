# Draft consumer pilot protocol

Status: draft; owner, privacy, and any required ethics review must be recorded
before recruitment.

Recruit 20–50 adults (18+) who already use Apple Health, Health Connect, or
Fitbit through wearable communities, university/workplace wellness groups, and
the existing research network. Do not recruit by implying depression detection
or clinical monitoring.

Milestones: onboarding; connection; baseline day 7; baseline day 14; week 4.
Collect voluntary surveys about comprehension, usefulness, burden, trust, and
deletion. Any diagnostics export must be shown to the participant and shared
only after a separate explicit action.

Predefined success thresholds:

- at least 80% health connection among consenters;
- at least 60% baseline completion;
- at least 40% week-4 return;
- at least 80% correctly identify Signa as wellness, not diagnosis;
- zero high-severity coach safety misses;
- fewer than 5% crash-blocked sessions.

Stop/pause rules: any privacy leak, clinical misunderstanding causing risk,
unrouted crisis/medication advice, repeated data-loss crash, or participant
request. Participation is voluntary and deletion is available in-app.
